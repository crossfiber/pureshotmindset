Level Up is a 365-day mental game program for golfers. A short guided meditation every day. A library of your own best shots. An incantation in your own voice. And a routine for every round you play: planned, played, and reviewed.

You hit it fine on the range. Then the first tee has trees left, water right, three people watching, and your practice swing belongs to somebody else.

That gap is not your swing. You have spent years training the swing and never a day training the thing that runs it under pressure: your attention. It trains the same way a swing does. A little of it, every day, on purpose.

Quote of the day

“The person I fear most in the last two rounds is myself.”
Tom Watson

Every day in the app opens with one. This is Day 1’s.

In for 4 Out for 6

“Sit comfortably and let the eyes soften. Breathe in for four and out for six, unhurried.” That is how Day 1 begins.

Never meditated? Day 1 assumes nothing.

Every session trains your attention on two anchors: things you can actually feel on a golf course.

BreathWeight & contact of the feetSensations in the handsThe visual fieldSoundsWind & temperatureFeel of the stroke

You sit with them for a few minutes a day. Slowly, your attention gets somewhere to go besides your score, so that on Saturday, standing over a downhill four-footer, it goes there instead.

Every day comes in 3, 7 and 13 minute versions. Pick the one your morning allows. The streak does not care which.

One session a day. All year.

Every square is a session. Months one through eleven run thirty days each; month twelve runs thirty-five. The app keeps the count for you: your streak, your days completed, how deep into the year you are. The only square you ever need to think about is the red one.

Day 1, in red, is where everyone starts. The anchor pairs rotate all year, so no session repeats the one before it, and what the other 364 hold stays between you and the program.

Built around the whole round, not just the swing.

A round is won three times: before it, during it, and after it. The program wraps all three, so the work you do on a quiet Tuesday is standing next to you on the first tee Saturday.

01

Plan the day

Answer a few quick questions: tee time, practice, gym, meditation, even breakfast. The app builds your whole schedule around the round, so the day carries you to the tee instead of scattering you on the way there.

02

Before you tee off

Breath work to settle you or switch you on, a short focus meditation, and a pre-round sheet: today’s focus cue, the score you are playing for, the challenge you expect, and your answer to it, written down before it finds you.

03

On the course

A pre-shot routine you have rehearsed against the clock until it is yours, one focus cue, and an honest mark on every shot: committed, or not. On practice days, six scored drills keep the work from lying to you.

04

After the round

Seven minutes of letting the score go. Then the review: your best recovery, the three shots worth keeping, and one lesson that travels to the next round. The score gets released. The learning gets kept.

And between rounds, the belief work.

Sensory visualization

A library of your own game: your most clutch shots, the rounds you are proudest of, and your top 10 accomplishments in golf. Proof of what you have already done, read back whenever belief needs a lift.

“I am the player”

An incantation of the golfer you are becoming: disciplined, patient, relentless. You stand up, say it out loud, and record it, so the voice in your head on the back nine is your own.
