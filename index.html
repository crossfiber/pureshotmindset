Your swing gets daily reps. Now your head does too.

Pure Shot is a 365-day mental game program for golfers. One short guided meditation every day, at 3, 7, or 13 minutes, your pick. Six scored practice drills. And a pre-shot routine you rehearse until it holds up with a card in your hand.

You hit it fine on the range. Then the first tee has trees left, water right, three people watching, and your practice swing belongs to somebody else.

That gap is not your swing. It is attention. And attention trains the same way a swing does: a little of it, every day, on purpose.

Quote of the day

“The person I fear most in the last two rounds is myself.”
Tom Watson

Every day in the app opens with one. This is Day 1’s.

In for 4 Out for 6

“Sit comfortably and let the eyes soften. Breathe in for four and out for six, unhurried.” That is how Day 1 begins.

Never meditated? Day 1 assumes nothing.

Every session trains your attention on two anchors: things you can actually feel on a golf course.

BreathWeight & contact of the feetSensations in the handsThe visual fieldSoundsWind & temperatureFeel of the stroke

You sit with them for a few minutes a day. Slowly, your attention gets somewhere to go besides your score, so that on Saturday, standing over a downhill four-footer, it goes there instead.

Every day comes in 3, 7 and 13 minute versions. Pick the one your morning allows. The streak does not care which.

One session a day. All year.

Months one through eleven run thirty days each. Month twelve runs thirty-five. The red squares are the six days pulled out on the right, sitting where they actually fall in the year. The app keeps the count for you: your streak, your days completed, how deep into the year you are.

The part with a club in your hand.

Meditation is half the program. The other half is a practice system that scores what you control: 20 shots at a time, about thirty minutes a drill. The app keeps your best score, your two-week average, and the number that matters most: how many of those shots you were fully committed over.

The routine timer

Write down your pre-shot routine: how you pick a target, how you prime your body, how many steps in, how many looks. Then rehearse it against the clock. First beep, first step toward the ball. Second beep, club goes back. Rep after rep, until the routine you use under pressure is the same one you use on a Tuesday.

The commitment tracker

Write one focus cue. Then mark every shot you hit: committed, or not. Two weeks of honest marks shows you the pattern, and the pattern is the thing you practice next.

Before you tee off

Four minutes of calming breath work, or three of energizing, your call, then a five-minute focus meditation. After that, the pre-round sheet: today’s focus cue, the score you are playing for, the challenge you expect, and your answer to it.
